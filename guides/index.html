Cooking methods

How to grill fishDirect high heat over open flame, best on firm fish thick enough to survive being turned.How to pan-sear fishA hot heavy pan and very little movement — the most reliable way to cook nearly anything in the case.How to roast fishDry oven heat — the least demanding method and the easiest one to walk away from.How to smok fishLow heat and wood smoke over hours — a preservation method that turned into a delicacy.How to poach fishGentle submersion in barely-moving liquid — the most controlled and least forgiving-of-neglect method.How to fry fishHot oil and a coating — the treatment most of the lean white fish in the case eventually gets.How to serving raw fishCrudo, sashimi, ceviche and poke — where handling quality is the entire dish.How to steam fishMoist heat in a covered pot — the method shellfish is built for.
