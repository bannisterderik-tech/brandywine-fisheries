Questions

Which is cheaper, Chinook salmon or steelhead?

Chinook Salmon is $29.95 / lb; Steelhead is Smoked, $40.00.

Which is easier to cook?

They're comparable. Chinook Salmon cooks to 125°F for medium-rare in the center, Steelhead to 125–130°F if you are cooking a fresh piece.

Can I substitute one for the other?

Yes — they are listed as substitutes for each other and behave similarly in the pan.

Which is the Oregon fish?

Both. Each is landed by the boat out of Charleston.
